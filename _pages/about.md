Welcome to my personal webpage! I am an M.Tech. (Research) student in Electrical Engineering at the [Indian Institute of Science (IISc), Bengaluru](https://iisc.ac.in/), where I work at the [SPIRE Lab](https://spire.ee.iisc.ac.in/) under the guidance of [Prof. Prasanta Kumar Ghosh](https://ee.iisc.ac.in/~prasantg/).

My research focuses on automatic speech recognition (ASR) and dialect identification (DID) for low-resource Indian languages. I am interested in how auxiliary tasks such as dialect and domain identification can be used to improve ASR, and in building reproducible pipelines for curating and quality-checking speech corpora at scale.

Before starting my M.Tech. (Research), I was a Research Associate at the SPIRE Lab (2021–2026) and a Machine Learning / Signal Processing Consulting Engineer at [ARTPARK](https://www.artpark.in/) @ IISc (2022–2026). I hold a B.Tech. in Electronics and Communication Engineering from the National Institute of Technology (NIT), Patna.

## Research Highlights

- **RESPIN-S1.0 (NeurIPS 2025):** First author of the [RESPIN-S1.0 database paper](https://openreview.net/forum?id=qL8M2dOY4L) at the NeurIPS 2025 Datasets and Benchmarks Track — a read speech corpus of 10,000+ hours across dialects of nine Indian languages. I led the large-scale data curation, reproducible benchmarking, and baseline ASR development.
- **Dialect identification:** Led the first large-scale study of DID in Indian languages (79.81% accuracy across 33 dialects), and developed a multimodal fusion model for joint ASR and DID reaching 4.65% CER with 81.63% DID accuracy.
- **Low-resource ASR:** Improved ASR through adapter-based domain adaptation (10–30% relative WER reduction across nine languages) and a gated multi-encoder model with frame-level gating (7.5% WER reduction on Bhojpuri, 9% on Bengali).
- **Speech synthesis (SYSPIN):** Built a Kaldi-based ASR data filtering pipeline that improved corpus quality for a text-to-speech system in nine Indian languages.
- **Data curation (VAANI):** Developed automated signal processing and machine learning pipelines for acoustic quality assessment, speech activity detection, and metadata validation, and established the acceptance criteria underpinning the open-source release of 31,000+ hours of speech from 156,000+ speakers across 109 languages.

## Challenges and Professional Service

I was the lead organizer of the **MADASR 2.0** Multi-Lingual Multi-Dialect ASR Challenge at IEEE ASRU 2025, where I designed the challenge tracks, evaluation protocols, and public leaderboards, and developed the ESPnet-based baseline systems. I have also co-organized **LIMMITS'24** (ICASSP 2024), **MADASR** (ASRU 2023), and the **Gram Vaani ASR Challenge** (Interspeech 2022).

I serve as a reviewer for ICASSP, IJCNN, Interspeech, IEEE ASRU and IEEE SLT, and for the journals *Computer Speech & Language* and *Speech Communication*. In 2022 I won the **Best Hackathon Project Award** at the SLT-CODE Hackathon for dialectal ASR systems in Bengali and Bhojpuri.
