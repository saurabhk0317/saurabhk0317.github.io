## Experience  

**Research Associate**  
*SPIRE Lab, Indian Institute of Science (IISc), Bengaluru, India*  
July 2021 – July 2026  
Advisor: Prof. Prasanta Kumar Ghosh  

*RESPIN: Speech Recognition in Agriculture and Finance for the Poor in India*
- First author of the RESPIN-S1.0 database paper published at **NeurIPS 2025 (Datasets and Benchmarks Track)**; led large-scale data curation, reproducible benchmarking, and baseline ASR development across nine Indian languages.  
- Developed a multimodal fusion model for joint ASR and dialect identification (DID), reaching **4.65% CER and 81.63% DID accuracy**, integrating speech and text embeddings for Indian dialects.  
- Led the first large-scale study on DID in Indian languages (**79.81% accuracy across 33 dialects**).  
- Enhanced low-resource ASR via adapter-based domain adaptation, reducing **WER by 10–30%** and **CER by 3–18%** across nine languages.  
- Proposed a Gated Multi-Encoder ASR model with frame-level gating, reducing **WER by 7.5% (Bhojpuri) and 9% (Bengali)**.  

*SYSPIN: Text-to-Speech Synthesizer in Nine Indian Languages*
- Developed a data filtering pipeline using Kaldi-based ASR models, improving corpus quality.  

**Machine Learning / Signal Processing Consulting Engineer**  
*ARTPARK @ IISc, Bengaluru, India*  
September 2022 – July 2026  

*VAANI: Capturing the language landscape for an inclusive digital India*
- Developed automated signal processing and machine learning pipelines for acoustic quality assessment, speech activity detection, and metadata validation.  
- Established quantitative acceptance criteria and automated corpus curation protocols underpinning the open-source release of **31,000+ hours** of speech from **156,000+ speakers** across **109 languages** and **31 states and union territories**.  

**Undergraduate Student Researcher**

July 2019 – June 2021  
Advisor: Dr. S. Shahnawazuddin  
- Investigated formant-modification and bispectrum-based front-end features to improve children's ASR under zero-resource conditions.  
- Developed a CGAN-based voice conversion framework, transforming adult speech into child-like speech for ASR adaptation.  

**Summer Intern**
